v2 · 9 screens × 1440 / 390 · direction 2b
Forge, done editorially. Warm graphite ground, one molten-orange light source. Orange is reserved for the single primary action and for "fresh" things (newest, done); everything else is cream and grey.
Type does the fun. Instrument Serif italic for the big moments (titles, Auto's status, empty states), Geist for reading, Geist Mono for tool lines and code. No rounded type, no candy colours.
Auto keeps his colours. Mint eyes and the page-drawn ring (mint calm, bronze busy, coral help, dusk asleep) are the only cool tones in the UI, so he always reads as alive. Yellow tags = motion notes; toggle in Tweaks.
