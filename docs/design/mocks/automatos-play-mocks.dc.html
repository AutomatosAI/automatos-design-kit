Automatos Play — mocks
v1 · dark theme · 9 screens × 1440 / 390
Direction. Night workshop: deep indigo ground, forge-bronze for the one primary action per screen, mint for Auto's light and "done". Fredoka for headings (rounded, game-launcher energy without going toddler), Figtree for reading, JetBrains Mono for tool lines and code.
Mascot. Every Auto is a slot: drop auto-hero.png in the project root and it appears everywhere. Until then a placeholder shows the state name. The ring is drawn by the page: mint = calm/happy, bronze = busy, coral = needs help, dusk = asleep/lost.
Assumptions. Only Chat, Conversations and Deliverables exist in nav (no Missions/settings per brief). Laptop prices and specs are example data. Yellow tags are motion/state notes — toggle them and the mascot off in Tweaks to check the page reads without him.
